Changes to the 2026-2027 Season

Primary Changes

  • Winner of the losers bracket gets rewarded.
  • Managers are determining their draft positions based on last year's results.
Explain that to me

Snake drafts the way we have them set up are not always ideal for the person who gets first pick. They get 1st and then 20th, when the person who has last pick gets the 10th and 11th picks, which is typically a lot better of an option.

Two things are happening.

  1. Instead of making our playoff results about 1st, 2nd, 3rd pick, we are focusing them on picking your draft positioning. This means instead of the worst team last year getting one good pick and then missing out on the majority of top tier players, they can make a decision to take a lower pick in order to get more good players instead of one really good player.
  2. In an effort to keep everyone engaged all the way through playoffs, we are giving the winner of the losers bracket, aka 5th place, the third draft position pick.

In this format, last place picks their position first, then second to last, then the winner of the losers bracket. That way people have something to play for at the end, and last place isn't screwed getting first pick and 20th pick. They can have picks closer together earlier if they want.

Instead of going for the first pick and getting 1st and 20th, you can go for 4th to get the 4th and 17th pick, or 8th to get the 8th and 13th pick. It allows for more strategy in the draft.
